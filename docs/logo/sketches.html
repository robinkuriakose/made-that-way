Made That Way: logo sketches

Three directions, each shown as the full logo, the small mark on light and dark, and in the top bar. Sketches, not finals: once you pick one, I draw it properly and it becomes the favicon too.

1. The bump

my pick

A keycap with the little ridge from the F and J keys: the detail your finger finds every day and nobody notices. It is the game in one picture, and it still reads as a key at 16 pixels.

3. The label

A woven clothing label, the kind that says "Made in India", saying "Made That Way" instead. Every object carries a label about where it came from; ours is about why. Strong as a sticker or a badge; weaker as a tiny icon.

Why the bump. It says what the game is about without words, it is ours (the F and J question is the model for every question), and it survives being tiny, which the favicon and the home screen icon on a phone need. The markup is the runner up: more hand-made, but the loop alone is harder to recognise small. The label is the best pun and the weakest icon.

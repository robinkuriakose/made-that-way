# V2 plan

From the test group (27 September 2026): developers and some product designers, average score 50%.

## What they said, and what V2 does about it

| They said | V2 |
|---|---|
| What is this for? The home screen says too much | One-line promise, today's question, one Start button. Everything else below the fold |
| Tired by questions 5 to 7 | Levels of 5 questions. After each level, a short break: your running score, a reward, "Level 2". The run carries on as long as you do |
| No reason to play again | Gamification: a running score across levels, combos for answers in a row, level-ups, rewards, a collection of "whys" you've uncovered, and a weekly leaderboard |
| Text-only questions are tiring | Runs use only questions with a picture, until the missing pictures arrive |
| Didn't understand the timer | A countdown with a number, and points you can see shrinking |
| Some read the reasons, few reacted | The reason appears right after each answer, one line, with "read more" |
| 50% average | Level 1 starts with the easier questions, so people build momentum |

## The owner's idea

Runs of 5. Then prompt "Level 2" (a word that makes people want more), and show the cumulative score, like an infinite run. Weekly leaderboard. Explore gamification: micro interactions, delight, rewards.

## Open questions (answer before building)

1. **When does the endless run end?**
   - Recommended: 3 lives, and a redeem earns a life back instead of points.
   - With a 50% average, 3 lives last about 6 questions (into level 2). A life for each level cleared would stretch that.
   - The alternative, "stop whenever you like", lets the leaderboard reward whoever plays longest rather than best.
2. **What makes a level different?** Recommended: each level is worth more (x1, x1.2, x1.5 and so on) and draws on slightly harder questions.
3. **Which rewards in V2?**
   - Recommended: combo multiplier, level-up moment, "whys uncovered" collection, and 4 badges (first level, perfect level, 3-day streak, 50 whys).
   - Not in V2: coins, shops, avatars.
4. **Timer:** keep 45 seconds with a visible number? Recommended: yes.
5. **Leaderboard:** weekly only, resetting on Monday, with your all-time best shown privately? Recommended: yes.
6. **Tidbits:** they trigger after 3 misses, which lives would already end. Recommended: show a tidbit at each level break instead.
7. **The one-line promise:** pick one, or write your own.
   - "Why is it made that way? Find out in 5 questions."
   - "Everyday things, explained. One question at a time."
   - "Spot the reason behind the design."
